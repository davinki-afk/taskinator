Custom sounds

  1. Open the folder containing Taskinator.exe. In the sounds folder next to it, create a subfolder for your sound pack, such as sounds\MySounds.
  2. Put your WAV files in that subfolder and name them as shown in the table.
  3. In Taskinator, open “Settings” → “Sounds”, click “Refresh list”, select your folder from the “Sound folder” list, and click “OK”.
File nameWhen it plays
taskcomplete.wavWhen you mark a task as complete
reminder.wavWhen an early reminder goes off
alarm.wavWhen a reminder reaches its scheduled time

You can replace any of the three sounds. If a file is missing from your sound pack or cannot be played, Taskinator tries to use the corresponding file from sounds\default.

If Taskinator is installed in Program Files, Windows may ask for administrator permission when you copy the files.
